the story

Artist history

1991

Born in Gladwin, Michigan

Nathan John Feuerstein is born on March 30, 1991, the eldest of three children. A difficult childhood in Gladwin shapes the confessional writing to come.

2010

Moments

Releases his independent debut Moments, laying the groundwork for his raw, cinematic style.

2015

Mansion

Major-label debut Mansion arrives March 31, introducing NF's signature sound to a wider audience.

2016

Therapy Session

Second studio album (Apr 22) climbs the charts and deepens his reputation for unflinching honesty.

2017

Perception & the crossover

Perception (Oct 6) goes to #1, and "Let You Down" reaches #12 on the Hot 100, eventually certified triple-platinum.

2019

The Search debuts at #1

The Search opens at #1 on the Billboard 200 and sends NF to #1 on the Artist 100 for the first time.

2021

CLOUDS

Drops CLOUDS (The Mixtape), home to fan favorites like "JUST LIKE YOU" and "The Motto."

2023

HOPE

Returns with HOPE (Apr 7), led by the single "HAPPY" — his most reflective record yet.

2024

The HOPE Tour

Takes the HOPE record on the road across spring 2024.

2025

FEAR EP

Releases the six-song FEAR EP (Nov 14) with mgk and James Arthur; it debuts at #4 and the title track hits a career-best #35 Hot 100 entry.
