05 · outline

          
The border that takes no space

          

            Same box, three modes. Add a border and the box grows —
            layout shifts. Add an outline and nothing moves: it draws
            outside, over whatever is there. That's why focus rings use
            it.

06 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 08

        

        

          

            

            
border vs outline

            

              Border takes space and follows the radius. Outline takes
              no space, draws outside, and can offset away from the box.
              Focus rings = outline.
            

            

              ↳ hover: the ring appears without moving anything
            

          

          

            

            
radius recipes

            

              50% = circle on a square.
              999px = pill on anything. Four values =
              four corners, clockwise from top-left.
            

            
↳ hover: square becomes circle

          

          

            

            
shadows stack

            

              Comma-separated, first written = topmost. Tight+dark for contact,
              wide+soft for ambient. Two shadows beat one loud shadow.
            

            
↳ hover: three layers combine

          

          

            

            
keep the ring

            

              :focus-visible + a custom outline =
              accessible and on-brand.
              outline: none with no replacement is a
              bug, not a style choice.
            

            
↳ hover: the focus ring, restyled
