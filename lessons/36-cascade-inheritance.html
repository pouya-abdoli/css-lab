The cascade decides. Styles flow down.

Two engines deliver every computed value. The cascade picks which rule wins when several conflict (importance → origin → specificity → order). Inheritance then passes chosen values from parent to child — for the properties that flow. Keywords like inherit, initial, unset and revert let you steer both.

01 · the cascade

Four steps to a winner

When declarations conflict, the cascade compares: importance (!important), then origin, then specificity, then source order — stopping at the first difference. Toggle rules on/off and watch which step decides.
