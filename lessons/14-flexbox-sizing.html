lesson 14 · interactive

Free space is a budget.

Flexbox sizes items in three moves: start at flex-basis, share leftover space with flex-grow, absorb overflow with flex-shrink. The flex shorthand writes all three at once — and gap handles the air between. This lesson makes the math visible.

01 · flex-basis

The starting size

Before any growing or shrinking, each item says "I start at this size". That's flex-basis. With grow & shrink off, the item insists on exactly its basis — even if it overflows.

06 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 14

        

        

          

            

              

            

            
basis = the opening bid

            

              The size an item starts at before negotiation.
              0 means "build me from shares only";
              auto means "measure my content first".
            

            
↳ hover: the bid grows

          

          

            

              

            

            
grow = appetite

            

              Shares of the free space, proportional not equal. All
              zeros? The leftover stays unused. Grow only matters when space
              exists.
            

            
↳ hover: the middle one gets greedy

          

          

            

              

            

            
shrink = politeness

            

              Who gives up space when things overflow.
              0 = rigid (logos, icons). Weighted by
              basis: bigger items sacrifice more.
            

            
↳ hover: two yield, one refuses

          

          

            

              

                
              

            

            
gap = civilized margins

            

              Space between items only — edges untouched, no collapsing, no
              :last-child margin resets. Works in flex
              and grid alike.
            

            
↳ hover: the grid breathes
