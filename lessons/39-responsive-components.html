lesson 39 · interactive

Components that hear their own box.

A responsive component adapts to the space it gets, not the viewport — a card in a sidebar should stack even on a desktop. That's container queries: every demo below reflows purely from @container as you drag its width. No viewport, no JS layout.

the card reflows as its box breathes…

01 · responsive navbar

Links collapse to a burger

Wide, the links sit inline. Narrow, they hide and a burger appears. The component decides based on its own width — drop it in a footer or a modal and it still behaves.

07 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 39

        

        

          

            

              
@container (max-width: …)

            

            
respond to the box

            

              Components should adapt to their container, not the viewport. A
              sidebar card must stack on desktop too.
            

            
↳ hover: the query glows

          

          

            

              

                
              

            

            
collapse, don't cram

            

              Navbars swap links for a burger; tables swap rows for cards.
              Hiding and re-labeling beats shrinking text to nothing.
            

            
↳ hover: the burger appears

          

          

            

              

            

            
columns collapse first

            

              Multi-column forms and grids drop to one column early. Keep tap
              targets ≥44px regardless of column count.
            

            
↳ hover: it stacks

          

          

            

              

            

            
shells re-flow, not hide

            

              Dashboards and sidebars re-orient their grid instead of deleting
              regions, so no content is ever lost on small boxes.
            

            
↳ hover: the rail drops
