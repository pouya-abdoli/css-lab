lesson 31 · interactive

Micro-motion is the polish.

The difference between "a page" and "a product" is a layer of small, purposeful motion: hovers that acknowledge, loaders that reassure, skeletons that pre-tell, modals that arrive. All of it is a few lines of CSS.

01 · hover animations

Acknowledge the cursor

One card, four layered responses: lift + shadow, a light sweep across the image, the arrow nudging, the underline drawing. Hover (or Tab) — every layer answers. Restraint is the skill: 200–300ms, transform & opacity only.

03 · skeleton loaders

          
Show the shape of what's coming

          

            A skeleton pre-draws the layout in shimmering grey, so the arrival
            of content feels like filling in, not jumping. Press fetch:
            skeleton shimmers for 1.2s, then the real card fades in on the same

04 · modal animations

          
Arrive with intention

          

            Overlay fades while the panel enters — scale, slide or zoom. Open
            and close it; notice closing is faster (exits should never linger).
            Animate transform + opacity only.

05 · dropdown animations

          
Grow from the trigger

          

            A menu should unfold from its button:
            transform-origin: top + scale/opacity, a
            slide, or a clip-path wipe. Toggle it and switch the entrance — the
            origin is what sells it.

06 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 31

        

        

          

            

            
hover = feedback

            

              Lift + shadow + one accent move, 200–300ms, transform/opacity
              only. More than that is circus; less feels dead.
            

            
↳ hover: it lifts

          

          

            

            
loaders reassure

            

              Spinner for unknown waits, bar for known progress, dots for
              compact spaces. ~1s per cycle reads calm.
            

            
↳ hover: it spins

          

          

            

              

            

            
skeletons pre-tell

            

              Grey blocks matching the incoming layout + a shimmer sweep.
              Content arrival becomes "filling in", not reflow shock.
            

            
↳ hover: the shimmer runs

          

          

            

            
enter slow, exit fast

            

              Modals & menus: ~300ms in with personality, ~200ms out
              without. Origins at the trigger make growth feel physical.
            

            
↳ hover: it pops in
