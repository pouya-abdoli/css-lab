lesson 29 · interactive

Move pixels, not layout.

Transforms repaint an element without reflowing the page — the slot it occupies never changes, only its drawn position, size, angle or depth. That's why they're cheap, GPU-friendly, and the backbone of motion design.

01 · translate

Move without reflow

translate(x, y) slides the element. The dashed ghost marks where it still lives in layout — neighbours never notice. This is how you animate position for free.

04 · basic 3D transforms

05 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 29

        

        

          

            

            
no reflow, ever

            

              Transforms repaint only; the layout slot never moves. That's why
              translate/scale/rotate are the only properties you animate in
              performance-critical UI.
            

            
↳ hover: it slides, slot stays

          

          

            

            
origin = the pin

            

              scale & rotate pivot around transform-origin. Same transform,
              different origin, completely different feel. Set it on purpose.
            

            
↳ hover: grows from the corner

          

          

            

            
order is the value

            

              rotate(skew(x)) ≠ skew(rotate(x)). Functions compose
              right-to-left. When a transform "looks wrong", it's usually the
              order.
            

            
↳ hover: spin with overshoot

          

          

            

            
perspective = depth

            

              On the parent. Small value = strong 3D; large = subtle.
              Without it, rotateY just looks like a squish.
            

            
↳ hover: the card turns
