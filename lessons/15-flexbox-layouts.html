Flexbox isn't a trick — it's a toolbelt.

Every layout you admire on the web is a handful of flexbox moves: a navbar is space-between, a hero is center + center, equal cards are stretch + margin-top:auto, a page shell is column + flex:1. This lesson builds all five, live.

01 · the navbar

Logo left, links right

The most common flexbox on earth. Three ways to place the link group: space-between on the container, margin-left:auto on the links (the "pusher"), or just center everything.

02 · centered content

          
The two-line centering spell

          

            Before flexbox, vertical centering was a dark art. Now:
            justify-content: center +
            align-items: center. Toggle each axis and

03 · equal cards

          
Stretch + the bottom pin

          

            Two moves: the row's default align-items: stretch makes every
            card as tall as the tallest; inside each card,
            margin-top: auto pins the button to the bottom. Add lines to
            the middle card and watch the others follow.

04 · header & footer

          
The sticky-footer shell

          

            Column flex with main { flex: 1 }: the main area becomes a
            sponge that absorbs all spare height, so the footer sits at the
            bottom even when the page is nearly empty. Add content — the footer
            never floats.

              flex:1 on main does the rest — no calc(), no magic numbers.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
05 · sidebar + main

          
Fixed rail, liquid content

          

            The classic two-column: sidebar gets flex: 0 0 160px (never
            grows, never shrinks), main gets flex: 1 (takes everything
            left). Drag the rail and watch main absorb the change instantly.

              Together they replace every float-based column hack ever written.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
06 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 15

        

        

          

            

              

            

            
navbar = space-between

            

              Logo left, links right, one property. Or push the links with
              margin-left: auto — same result,
              different mental model.
            

            
↳ hover: the links get pushed

          

          

            

              

            

            
center = two props

            

              justify-content: center + align-items: center. The whole
              vertical-centering war, ended in two lines.
            

            
↳ hover: the box finds home

          

          

            

              

            

            
cards = stretch + pin

            

              stretch equalizes heights; margin-top: auto pins the button.
              Uneven content, perfectly aligned feet.
            

            
↳ hover: heights equalize

          

          

            

              

            

            
columns = 0 0 X + 1

            

              Sidebar flex: 0 0 Xpx, main flex: 1. The rail stays rigid, the
              content stays liquid — resize-proof by construction.
            

            
↳ hover: the rail widens
