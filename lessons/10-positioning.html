Position answers: where do I sit?

Normal flow is a queue. position lets an element leave it — shift from its seat (relative), pin to a parent (absolute), pin to the screen (fixed), or ride along until a threshold (sticky). Four offsets — top, right, bottom, left — do the steering.

06 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 10

        

        

          

            

            
static ignores offsets

            

              The default. top/left/right/bottom do nothing to a static
              box — it just stands in the queue. Every element starts here.
            

            
↳ hover: the push that never works

          

          

            

              

            

            
relative keeps its seat

            

              Offsets nudge the box visually, but the layout remembers where it
              was — neighbours don't move. The dashed seat stays warm.
            

            
↳ hover: nudge with a ghost

          

          

            

              

            

            
absolute needs an anchor

            

              It pins to the nearest positioned ancestor — usually a
              parent you set to relative on purpose.
              No anchor? It climbs all the way to the page.
            

            
↳ hover: jump to the other corner

          

          

            

              

                

                
              

            

            
sticky & fixed

            

              Fixed = pinned to the viewport forever. Sticky = relative until a
              threshold, then fixed inside its parent. Both live outside
              normal flow while pinned.
            

            
↳ hover: the bar slides then pins
