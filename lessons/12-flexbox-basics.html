lesson 12 · interactive

One line turns a pile into a team.

display: flex is the switch. The parent becomes a flex container, its children become flex items, and suddenly two axes exist: the main axis (where items flow) and the cross axis (perpendicular). That's the whole idea — everything else is alignment.

03 · the main axis

          
Where the items flow

          

            flex-direction points the main axis. Items always line up
            along it, from main-start to main-end. Flip the

04 · the cross axis

          
Always perpendicular

          

            The cross axis has no property of its own — it simply exists at 90°
            to the main axis. Change the direction and watch the orange arrow
            swap. Alignment along it (align-items) is
            lesson 13's job.

one line, new physics

            

              display: flex on the parent is all it

container vs items

            

              Container props (direction, justify, align, gap) layout the group.
              Item props (grow, shrink, basis, align-self) tune individuals.
              Wrong toolbox = "why isn't this working?".
            

            
↳ hover: the frame takes charge

          

          

            

              
123

            

            
main axis = flow

            

              flex-direction points it. Items run main-start → main-end.
              row-reverse & column-reverse flip the flow without touching
              the HTML order.
            

            
↳ hover: the flow reverses

          

          

            

            
cross = 90°

            

              No property defines it — it just exists perpendicular to main.
              justify-* rides the main axis, align-* rides the cross. Memorize
              that pairing and lesson 13 writes itself.
            

            
↳ hover: the arrows swap
