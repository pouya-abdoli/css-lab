Style the parts that aren't there.

Pseudo-elements (double colon, ::) reach inside or outside an element: generated content with ::before / ::after, editorial typography with ::first-letter / ::first-line, and the user's own marks with ::selection / ::placeholder. Six tools, zero extra HTML.

02 · ::first-letter

          
The drop cap

          

            Centuries of print typography in one selector: enlarge and float the
            first letter. Drag the size, pick the ink. Toggle it off to see the
            paragraph forget its manners.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
straight out of a magazine

            

            

              

                Tuesday is roast day at Aurora. The drum spins up at six, the
                first crack sounds like applause, and by noon the whole street
                smells of caramel and stone fruit. We bag it warm, date it
                honestly, and ride it to your door before the weekend.
              

            

          

          

            

              
              
            

            

              

                3em
              

              
            

            

              
            

            

            

              float: left is what lets the text wrap around it. Only works on
              block containers — not on flex or grid items.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
03 · ::first-line

          
The line that knows your width

          

            ::first-line styles wherever line one happens to end — which
            depends on the container's width. Drag the width and watch the amber
            uppercase portion re-wrap. No other selector is this alive.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
drag the width

            

            

              

                

                  Small-batch beans, roasted every Tuesday and shipped within 24
                  hours. No subscriptions, no nonsense — just very good coffee,
                  delivered before the weekend, wrapped in paper not plastic.
                

              

              

            

          

          

            

              

                440px
              

              
            

            

            

              Resize the browser and it recomputes too. That's the point: you
              style a region of text, not a fixed set of words.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
04 · ::selection

          
Brand the highlighter

          

            When users drag across text, the browser paints a selection — and
            you choose the paint. Pick colors, then select the paragraph.
            Keep the contrast readable: people select to copy, not to admire.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
drag across me

            

            

              

                Select this sentence with your mouse or Shift+arrows. The
                highlight you're painting right now is ::selection — the only
                pseudo-element the user triggers by reading.
              

              

            

          

          

            

              
            

            

              
            

            

            

              Set it on ::selection globally for a
              site-wide signature. Limited properties on purpose: color,
              background, and a few text styles.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
05 · ::placeholder

          
The ghost in the input

          

            The hint text is styleable too. Write the hint, tint it, fade it —
            but never below readable, and remember lesson 20: a placeholder is a
            hint, not a label. It vanishes the moment someone types.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
type in it — the ghost leaves

            

            

              
              

                the placeholder styles the hint, not the typed value
              

            

          

          

            
hint text

              Browsers default placeholders to ~0.54 opacity. Setting opacity: 1
              with a muted color is the accessible move.
