lesson 21 · interactive

Accessible isn't a feature. It's the baseline.

Millions navigate by keyboard, screen reader, or with low vision. The focus ring is their cursor; contrast is their legibility; reduced motion is their vestibular safety. This lesson makes all three visible — and testable.

T · AB · S · EE · H · EAR
no ring — keyboard users are lost

one button, four focus philosophies…

01 · :focus

The element that's "listening"

Exactly one element at a time holds focus — the one receiving keyboard input. Click or Tab through the row; the :focus ring follows, and the readout names whoever holds it. Mouse clicks focus too — that's the catch.

              :focus fires for every focus method — mouse, touch,
              keyboard. Great for inputs (always show a ring while typing),
              noisy for buttons clicked by mouse. That's what :focus-visible
              fixes.

03 · keyboard navigation

          
Tab order = DOM order

          

            The Tab key walks focusable elements in document order —
            nothing more. Simulate Tab (or really Tab into the stage) and watch
            the numbered path. Shuffle the DOM and the path reshuffles: order is
            markup, not pixels.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
follow the numbers

              Positive tabindex values (tabindex="3")
              break this natural order — avoid them. tabindex="0" adds an
              element to the flow; "-1" removes it.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
04 · accessible contrast

          
The 4.5 : 1 rule, live

          

            WCAG asks for 4.5:1 on normal text (AA), 3:1 on large
            text, 7:1 for AAA. Pick any two colors and the meter tells
            you instantly who passes — no more guessing pastel-on-pastel.

05 · prefers-reduced-motion

          
Motion is not mandatory

          

            Vestibular disorders make parallax and bounce genuinely nauseating.
            The OS exposes a setting; this media query honors it. Flip the
            simulated switch — the bounce and pulse stop instantly.

              stylesheet. Respect it for scroll animations, parallax, and
              autoplaying carousels too.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
06 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 21

        

        

          

            

              
            

            
the ring is a cursor

            

              Keyboard users see focus the way mouse users see a pointer.
              outline: none without a replacement is
              removing someone's cursor.
            

            
↳ hover: the ring appears

          

          

            

              

            

            
order = markup

            

              Tab walks the DOM, not the layout. If the visual order and tab
              order disagree, screen-reader-and-keyboard users get lost.
              Grid/flex order can desync them — check.
            

            
↳ hover: focus steps forward

4.5 : 1 or it ships not

            

              AA normal text 4.5:1, large text 3:1, AAA 7:1. Test palettes
              before you fall in love with them — contrast math doesn't
              negotiate.
            

            
↳ hover: gray-on-gray becomes legible

          

          

            

            
motion = opt-out-able

            

              @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)
              is three lines that make your site safe for vestibular disorders.
              Ship it in every project.
