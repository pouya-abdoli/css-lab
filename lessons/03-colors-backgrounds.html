04 · hsl / hsla

          
HSL — the human's language

          

            Hue is a place on the color wheel (0–360°).
            Saturation is intensity. Lightness is how bright. Want
            "the same blue, but calmer"? Lower the saturation. Try that in hex.

three languages, one color

            

              Hex for design tools, rgb for machines and alpha, hsl for humans.
              The browser reads all three identically — pick whichever you can
              reason about.
            

            
↳ hover: the translations arrive

          

          

            

            
alpha needs a backdrop

            

              Transparency only means something over something. That's
              why design tools show a checkerboard behind transparent colors —
              and why your rgba() overlay needs content under it.
            

            
↳ hover: opacity solidifies

          

          

            

            
gradients are images

            

              Not colors — images. So they use background-image, can
              layer with other backgrounds, and obey size / position / repeat
              just like any picture.
            

            
↳ hover: the gradient travels

          

          

            

              background: #0d1220 url("cup.svg")
                center / cover no-repeat;
            

            
the shorthand

            

              Color, image, position, size and repeat can collapse into one
              background: line. Read it left to right
              and it's the same five controls.
            

            
↳ hover: the one-liner lights up
