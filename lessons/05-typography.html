02 · font-size

          
Size sets the hierarchy

04 · font-style

          
normal, italic, oblique

          

            italic is a true cursive design (or should be).
            oblique is just the regular glyphs slanted.
            normal turns both off.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
three attitudes

            

            

              

                

                  The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
                

              

            

          

          

            

              
              
              
              
            

            

            

              Use italic for emphasis & citations — never for whole
              paragraphs. Slanted text at length is tiring to read.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
05 · line-height

          
The lungs of a paragraph

          

            Too tight and lines collide; too loose and the paragraph dissolves.
            The sweet spot for body text is 1.5–1.7 — and
            unitless, so it scales with the font. The readout tells you
            when you're in the zone.

07 · text-align

          
Where the line ends

          

            Left is the reading default. Center is for short, ceremonial text.
            Right is for accents. Justify sounds professional but punches
            uneven gaps ("rivers") into ragged widths — try it.
