01 · justify-content

Distribute along the main axis

When the items don't fill the line, justify-content decides where the free space goes: packed at a start, centered, or shared between/around/evenly. It follows the main axis — flip the direction and it follows that too.

One item rebels

how it works

            

            

              Values: auto → flex-start → center → flex-end → stretch (click

              of a navbar with margin-left: auto's

05 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 13

        

        

          

            

              

            

            
justify = main axis

            

              Distributes free space along the flow: start, center, end,
              between, around, evenly. Remember: it does nothing if items
              already fill the line.
            

            
↳ hover: space goes even

          

          

            

              

            

            
align-items = cross

            

              Places items on the perpendicular axis. stretch equalizes heights,
              baseline aligns the text inside the boxes — the designer's
              favourite.
            

            
↳ hover: boxes find center

          

          

            

              

                
              

            

            
align-content = lines

            

              Only wakes up with wrapped lines + spare space. It moves whole
              rows, not items. Single-line flexbox? This property stays asleep.
            

            
↳ hover: lines spread apart

          

          

            

              

            

            
align-self = rebel

            

              Per-item override of align-items. One item can sit at the end
              while the group hugs the start. Small property, big layout tricks.
            

            
↳ hover: the middle one defects
