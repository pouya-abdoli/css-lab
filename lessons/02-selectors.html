06 · descendant & child

          
The family tree: space vs >

          

            A space means "anywhere inside, at any depth". A

class vs id

            

              Classes repeat — on many elements, and many per element. IDs are
              one-per-page landmarks. When in doubt, use a class.

the three tiny words

            

              Comma, space, greater-than. Three characters, three completely
              different meanings. Mix them up and your styles land somewhere
              unexpected.
