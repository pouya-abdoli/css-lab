lesson 38 · interactive

Every page is a few patterns.

Navbars, sidebars, heroes, cards, pricing tables, dashboards, footers, logins — nearly every site is the same eight layouts wearing different skins. Learn the flex/grid skeleton behind each and you can build almost anything.

S · AME · B · ONES · N · EW · SKIN

eight skeletons, infinite skins…

01 · navbar

Logo left, actions right

A flex row with margin-left: auto (or space-between) pushes the links to the right. The same markup stacks on small screens.

row vs stacked


            

              The auto-margin trick is the navbar's whole secret: it absorbs all
              free space, shoving everything after it to the edge.

04 · cards

          
auto-fit does the counting

          

            repeat(auto-fit, minmax(140px, 1fr)) fits
            as many cards as the row allows and stretches them to fill. No media
            queries, no manual column math.

Split screen, or centered card

09 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 38

        

        

          

            

              

            

            
auto-margin = navbar

            

              One margin-left: auto shoves the links
              right. Stack it with a media query and the navbar is done.
            

            
↳ hover: the cta slides

          

          

            

              

            

            
fixed rail + 1fr

            

              Sidebars and app shells are a fixed track plus a liquid one.
              Collapse by shrinking the first track, not by hiding content.
            

            
↳ hover: the rail narrows

          

          

            

              

            

            
pop with scale()

            

              Equal grid columns + a scaled highlight = pricing tables.
              Transform lifts without disturbing neighbours.
            

            
↳ hover: the middle pops

          

          

            

              

            

            
one grid, two layouts

            

              Split = 1fr 1fr; centered = 1fr. Most "different pages" are the
              same skeleton with a column count changed.
            

            
↳ hover: it collapses
