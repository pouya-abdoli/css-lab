lesson 20 · interactive

Forms are the web's conversation.

Every signup, search and checkout is a form — and unstyled form controls look like 1996. This lesson dresses all seven: inputs, buttons, labels, textareas, selects, checkboxes, radios — with the modern tricks (accent-color, appearance: none) that make them yours.

01 · inputs

The text field, tailored

Four properties do most of the work: padding (hit area), border, radius, and a :focus ring. Click the field — the focus glow is part of the design, not an afterthought.

03 · labels

          
The invisible usability win

          

            A <label for> turns its text into a
            remote control for the input — click the word "name" and the
            field focuses. Bigger target, happier thumbs, screen-reader support
            for free.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
click the label, not the box

              Top labels survive narrow screens and zoom; left labels save
              vertical space in dense desktop forms. Both need
              for + id.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
04 · textareas

          
The resizable one

          

            Textareas ship with a drag handle. The resize property
            decides which directions users may pull: both, vertical-only (the
            sane default), horizontal-only, or none. Grab the corner and test.
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
drag the corner

            

            

              

                
                
              

            

          

          

            

              
              
              
              
            

            

            

              Pair with min-height / max-height so
              dragging can't produce a 40px sliver or a page-eating monster.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
05 · selects

          
Taming the dropdown

          

            The select is the stubborn one: native chrome resists styling. The
            trick is appearance: none — erase the OS arrow, then draw
            your own with a background SVG and padding. Option lists stay native
            (and that's fine).
          

        

        

          

            

              
              
native vs custom

            

            

              

                
                
              

            

          

          

            

              
              
              
            

            

            

              Keep the native focus ring or replace it deliberately. Styling
              <option> is limited everywhere —
              don't fight it.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
06 · checkboxes & radios

          
One line, or full couture

          

            Level 1: accent-color themes the native controls in one

07 · field notes

          
Cheat codes from lesson 20

        

        

          

            

            
states are the design

            

              :hover, :focus, :active, :checked, :disabled — a form feels
              designed when every state was decided on purpose. Focus rings most
              of all.
            

            
↳ hover: the ring arrives

          

          

            

              
label = bigger target

            

            
labels = usability

            

              for + id makes the text clickable and the control announceable.
              Placeholder is a hint, never a label — it vanishes on input.
            

            
↳ hover: the pair connects

          

          

            

              

            

            
accent-color first

            

              One line themes checkboxes, radios, even range sliders — natively
              accessible. Reach for appearance: none only when the brand demands
              couture.
            

            
↳ hover: the check gets drawn

          

          

            

              
            

            
buttons need feedback

            

              cursor: pointer, a hover lift, an active press. A button that
              doesn't react to touch feels broken even when it works.
            

            
↳ hover: it lifts
